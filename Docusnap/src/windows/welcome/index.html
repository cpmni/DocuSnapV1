Welcome to Scan Finder

Scan Finder turns your scanned and saved documents into a tidy, searchable filing system — automatically. Drop in your invoices, orders and notes, and it reads the important details and files them away for you.

This quick tour takes about a minute. You can skip it any time.

How it works

When you bring in a document, Scan Finder reads it, picks out the key details — who it’s from, the reference number, the date, the total — and files it into neat folders by company, year and month. You stay in control: anything it’s unsure about waits for you to check.

It all happens on this computer. Your documents never leave the machine and nothing goes to the cloud.

One document type, many layouts

Every supplier’s invoice looks different, but they’re all still invoices. So you set up a small handful of document types — like Invoice or Dispatch Note — not one for every company you deal with.

Scan Finder learns each new layout the first time it sees it, then recognises it from then on. A few types cover everything you receive.

Teach it where to look

If Scan Finder reads something wrong, or a brand-new layout turns up, you just draw a box around the correct value — the supplier name, the total, the date — and it remembers where that detail sits on that layout.

You teach it once. Next time a document from that supplier comes in, it knows exactly where to look.

Check and Confirm

Documents that need a second look land in Review. Glance over the details, fix anything that’s off, and click Confirm — that files the document and teaches Scan Finder, so it gets smarter every time.

Documents it’s fully confident about file themselves automatically — you’ll only spend time on the ones that actually need you.

You’re ready to go

That’s it. Bring in your first batch of documents from the Import screen, and let Scan Finder take it from there. The more you use it, the sharper it gets at recognising your suppliers and layouts.

Your filed documents land in your chosen folder — you can open it any time from the Home screen. Need more detail? The full User Guide is in the Help menu.
