User Guide

Use this page when a document needs a PAID stamp, someone's sign-off — or a colleague has sent one to you.

Stamps, approvals and the Mailbox

Once a document is filed, you can mark it the way you'd mark paper: press a stamp on it, or send it to a colleague for their approval. The original PDF is never touched — the stamp goes on a recorded copy, and every stamp and decision is kept with the document's history.

Stamp a document yourself

  1. Find the document in Search and click Stamp… (in Document Actions).
  2. Pick a stamp — PAID, APPROVED, RECEIVED… or make your own with + New stamp (a word and a colour).
  3. Move the stamp where you want it on the page, click to drop it, then Place stamp.

How you know it worked: the document shows a stamp badge, and a Stamped / Original switch lets you view either version. Each stamp is numbered and dated, and stamps add up over time — like a paper document passing across desks.

Who can stamp

Stamping is a per-person permission an admin grants in Settings → Users & activity. If you don't see a Stamp button, you don't have it yet. The stamps themselves (the words and colours on offer) are managed on the same tab.

Send a document to someone

With the office add-on, Send… sits beside Stamp. Choose why you're sending it:

  • Needs their approval — it lands in their Mailbox with Approve / Reject buttons. An approval is permanent and stamped with their name; a rejection asks them for a note saying why.
  • Just for information — they read it and press Got it.

You can add a note, see the state ("Sent to Bob — awaiting their approval"), and Cancel route if you change your mind before they act. Only people allowed to stamp can be asked to approve — the recipient list shows who qualifies.

The Mailbox

The Mailbox (a button in Search, and the Waiting on you card on Home) has four boxes: Inbox (waiting on you), Sent (waiting on others), Assigned and Completed. A rejected item comes back to your Sent box with the reason, and a Send again button once you've put it right.

Automatic routing (rules)

An admin can set rules so documents route themselves: "When an Invoice is filed and it's £500 or more, send it to Alex for approval." Built in Settings → Users & activity → Document routing — and Show me what this would do previews a rule against your recent documents before it goes live.

What's recorded

Every stamp, send, approval and rejection is written to the document's history and the audit log — who, when, and the note they left. The original file is never altered; the stamped copy is stored alongside it.
