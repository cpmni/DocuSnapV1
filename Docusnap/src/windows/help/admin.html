Scan Finder — User Guide
User Guide

Use this page if you're the person who set Scan Finder up — accounts, the licence, backups and the record of what's been done.

Users, licence and safekeeping

Everything here lives in Settings, below the Administration divider. Day to day you'll rarely visit; it matters when someone joins, something needs signing off, or you're moving computers.

People and what they're allowed to do

Settings → Users & activity:

  • Add a person with one of three roles — Admin (everything), Edit (import, review, file), Read only (search and view). They get a temporary password and choose their own at first sign-in.
  • Allowed to stamp is granted per person — it controls both stamping and being asked to approve documents. See Stamps and approvals.
  • Document stamps — the words on offer (PAID, APPROVED…); add your own here.
  • Document routing — rules that send a filed document to someone automatically.
  • Recent Activity — sign-ins and account changes, read-only.

The audit log

Settings → Audit is the full record of what happened — who filed, deleted, restored, printed, stamped or changed what, and when. Filter by person, document or kind of action, and expand a row for the detail. It's a record, not a control: nothing here changes anything, and values are kept free of document contents.

Licence and trial

Settings → Licensing shows your current state — trial days left, or the licence and which computer it's tied to — with Activate a licence key when you have one. Scan Finder works fully offline; if the licence screen ever blocks the app, it tells you exactly why and what to do. Client seats for the office add-on are counted on the same tab.

Backup & Restore

Settings → Advanced. One password-protected file holds your whole setup — document types, folders, naming rules and everything Scan Finder has learned. Export it after any big setup change and keep it (and the password) safe. Restore brings it back after a reinstall or on a new computer. Two honest limits: the backup does not contain your filed documents (they're ordinary files in your output folder) or your user accounts, and restoring overwrites the current setup — it can't be undone.

Diagnostics and the Terms

  • Diagnostic logging (Advanced) is off by default. Turn it on only if you're chasing a problem — the log file includes text read from your documents, so treat it accordingly.
  • Help improve Scan Finder is a separate, optional choice — anonymous counts only, with a View… button showing exactly what would be sent. Off by default.
  • View Terms & Disclaimer… (Advanced, and in the About box) re-opens the terms you accepted at first run.
  • Re-run setup wizard… walks the first-run questions again — handy after moving the output folder.
If someone's locked out

An admin can reset any password from the Users list. If the admin is locked out, the sign-in screen accepts the admin recovery code you were shown (and asked to keep) when the account was created.
