Scan Finder — User Guide
User Guide

Use this page when a sender starts filing by itself, or something was filed you didn't see.

When it files without asking — and how to take one back

Once Scan Finder has seen enough correct documents from a sender, it stops asking about that sender and files them for you. This is the point of the whole thing — but it always shows you what it did, and you can undo any of it.

How a sender earns it

Every time you confirm a document without changing anything, that's a clean tick for its sender. After enough clean ticks, the sender crosses the pass mark and starts filing by itself. The count beside the sender's name in Review counts down to it.

How sure it must be before it files without asking is a slider in Settings → Processing — the “filing by itself” section. Raise it to be more cautious, lower it to file more on its own.

“N more from this sender — file them?”

When you confirm one document, Scan Finder may notice others from the same sender waiting behind it and offer: “N more from <sender> — file them?” Click Yes and it files those too, the same way it just filed yours. Ignore the bar and nothing else moves.

Seeing what it filed by itself

The Home screen shows “N filed by itself” — click it to see the list. Nothing is ever hidden from you; a document filed on its own is one click from view.

Taking one back

Filed under the wrong company, or shouldn't have filed at all? Open it and click Put back. It returns to Review for you to fix, and Scan Finder treats that as a sign to be more careful with that sender. A filed document is never stuck.

Optional extras

Two aids are off until you turn them on in Settings: the activity strip (a live line of recent filings across the top of Review) and the Quick check grid (thumbnails of what filed by itself, to skim at a glance). Turn them on if you like to watch filings go by.
