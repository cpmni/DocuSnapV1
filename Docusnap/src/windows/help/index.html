User Guide

Welcome to Scan Finder

Think of Scan Finder as a filing clerk who learns the companies you deal with. Show it one example of each document you get, and it files the rest — asking you only when it isn't sure, and always showing you what it filed on its own.

Everything happens on this computer. No documents are uploaded or sent anywhere.

Who it's for

Anyone who deals with batches of scanned paperwork — invoices, orders, worksheets — and wants them read, named and filed without typing them out. You need no technical knowledge.

People sign in with one of three roles:

  • Admin — full access, including Settings and teaching new documents.
  • Edit — can import, review and file documents.
  • Read only — can search and view filed documents, but not change them.
