Scan Finder — User Guide
User Guide

Welcome to Scan Finder

Scan Finder turns folders of scanned documents into tidy, searchable, filed records — the date, the reference, the supplier, the total — so you don't have to type them out or hunt for paperwork later.

It reads each scan, pulls out the details that matter, lets you check anything it wasn't sure about, then files a clean, neatly-named copy. Everything happens on this computer. No documents are uploaded or sent anywhere.

Quick start — your first filed document

  1. Open Import from the nav rail and choose a folder that contains your scans.
  2. Click Process Documents and wait while Scan Finder reads them.
  3. Open Review from the nav rail to see anything that needs a quick check.
  4. For each document, glance at the preview, fix any field that looks wrong, and pick the document type.
  5. Click Confirm & File. Your document is saved, neatly named, in your output folder — find it again any time from Search.

Who Scan Finder is for

Anyone who deals with batches of scanned paperwork — invoices, orders, worksheets — and wants them read, named and filed without manual data entry. You don't need any technical knowledge to use it.

Depending on how your copy is set up, people sign in with one of three roles:

  • Admin — full access, including Settings and teaching new document layouts.
  • Edit — can import, review and file documents.
  • Read only — can search and view filed documents, but not change them.
