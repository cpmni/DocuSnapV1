## THE "overall-84" PENALTY — DIAGNOSED + FIXED (owner asked to dig in; `3f64c10`)
**Why a CORRECT Pelican invoice reads overall 84:** its `invoice_number` validation_note is counted as a
format MISMATCH by `validator.format_consistency_delta` → **−12** (base overall ~96 − 12 = 84).
**The real bug:** when a demoter clears that note, the recompute guard (engine.py ~9116) REUSED the STALE
`fc_delta` computed BEFORE the demote — so **every note-demoter (these arms AND the shipped
recon/name/xcheck slices) was COSMETIC**: the note cleared but overall kept the −12 and the doc stayed
below-floor. **Fix — `corrob_note_recompute_fc` (DEFAULT OFF, mig 69 + bridge + toggle + wiring pin):** when
armed, recompute `format_consistency_delta` off the POST-demote results so a cleared note actually lifts
confidence. Verified in isolation: doc 1716 base overall 96, notes cleared → fresh delta 0 → overall 96
(stored 84). Pinned (note→−12, cleared→0, guard recomputes gated by the flag). **This is the linchpin — it
is what makes ALL note-demoters (incl. the owner's existing-ON slices) actually reduce the review pile,
not just tidy wording.**

**BUT — the compounding blocker (why the fc fix alone does NOT auto-file Pelican):** `scopeTrust(Pelican,
invoice)` = `{trusted:false, floor:100, reason:'recent-correction', corrections:3}`. Graduation is
DELIBERATELY blocked while a scope has recent corrections (a stability safety), so Pelican's floor is 100 —
even at overall 96, `96 < 100` → held. **To auto-file the Pelican I/1 docs, BOTH must happen: (1) the
fc-recompute flip (84→96), AND (2) Pelican's scope must GRADUATE (floor 100→95) — which needs the 3 recent
corrections to age out.** The corroboration_autofile route can't rescue it either: Pelican's `invoice_number`
is single-method-read (`independent_agree:false`), so `_docFullyCorroborated` fails. So the honest position:
the arc + fc fix make note-demotes CORRECT + confidence-lifting on GRADUATED scopes; Pelican is held by a
graduation safety (by design), not a bug, and clears as the scope stabilises. Chris's #1 (the graduation /
auto-file bar) is the real lever there.
