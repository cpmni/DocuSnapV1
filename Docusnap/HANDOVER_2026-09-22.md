## ⚠ THE GOTCHA that cost the most time (read before the NEXT batch flip)
Many older per-key seed pins test "key not in `ALL_ON_DEFAULTS_93`" with a **loose regex**
`ALL_ON_DEFAULTS_93 = \[[\s\S]*?'key'[\s\S]*?\];` that scans from the array open to the **next `];` anywhere in
the file**. A batch `const flip = [ … ];` array — AND even a **comment that literally contains `];`** — gives
that regex a token to latch onto, so every seed pin whose key sits below `ALL_ON_DEFAULTS_93` (line 241)
false-positives. Fixes applied: mig 205 iterates `for (const k of [ … ])` (closes `])`, not `];`), and its
comment is worded to contain no bracket-semicolon token. If you add another batch flip, keep both rules.
