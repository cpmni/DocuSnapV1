## Finding cards (new this round, ranked by harm)

**1. Three dialects of "ready", one screen, contradicting each other**
- **Citation (verbatim):** Review chips "**3 more ready to file**" and "**7 more ready to file**" visible at the same moment as "**✓ Nothing filed — 58 kept back**" and "**Nothing is ready to file yet — every document in the queue is waiting on a check or a missing detail.**" (step22 screenshot shows all four). Group heads add: "**2 more to file by itself**" (Thornfield) and "**2 more credit note · 5 more invoice to file**" on a 6-document Helix group.
- **User-moment:** deciding whether pressing the big green File All Ready button was safe.
- **Observed confusion:** I read "7 more ready to file" and expected File All Ready to file 7. It filed 0. And 2+5=7 on a group of 6 — I can't make the arithmetic work anywhere.
- **Harm + severity:** trust-eroded — after one contradiction I stopped believing the chips, which also means I'd stop noticing them when they matter.
- **Class:** CONFUSION.
- **Proposed alternative:** one word per meaning. If the chip means "filled in but each needs your once-over", say "**7 filled in — each needs one check**". Keep "ready to file" only for what File All Ready would actually file.
- **What I may be missing:** the chips may be counting documents that become fileable *after* I confirm the new reads — but then the words should say that.

**2. Heading words land in the reference box dressed as answers**
- **Citation (verbatim):** Invoice Number = "**Date**" with "**⟳ Found on a second look — check it, then Confirm to keep.**" (seen on 6 of the ~20 docs I opened); and a swept credit note's number = "**NOTE**" at "**High · 70%**" with "**✓ auto-corrected — The taught position and the full-page read disagreed — using the full-page value; please verify.**"
- **User-moment:** scanning a held document's details to see what needs fixing.
- **Observed confusion:** the box contains a word off the page's furniture, not a reference — yet the note invites me to "Confirm to keep" it, and the "NOTE" one wears a tick and the word High. On a tired Friday I'd keep one.
- **Harm + severity:** slowed always; a real misfile if ever confirmed in bulk (File All refused these today — good).
- **Class:** CONFUSION.
- **Proposed alternative:** when the found value is a bare page word (Date, Total, Ref, NOTE), say "**This looks like a heading, not a reference — leave it blank or type the right one**", and never pair it with ✓/High styling.
- **What I may be missing:** maybe showing its best guess beats showing nothing; but the guess shouldn't out-dress the doubt.
