## 1. The belt — what it is, in plain terms (tell the owner in these words)
When a scan is cut in the wrong place because page 2 repeats the letterhead, the app used to file page 1 on its own and leave page 2 stranded.
Now it notices when the second page has no number and no date of its own, or repeats the first page's number, and holds both pages for one look
with a note that names the other page and says where the original scan is kept. A second page with its own number AND its own date is a separate
document and the first page files as normal. The owner's real 34-page Print Tracker alert bundle is never touched (every alert page has an email
header, so none of its cuts are "letterhead-only"). It is OFF by default (a dark switch) until the flip gate below is met.
